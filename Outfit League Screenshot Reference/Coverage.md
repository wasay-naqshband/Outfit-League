# Screenshot coverage

25 captures of the live Outfit League web app at 390 × 844. Filenames identify each screen. The five primary tabs are Explore, Try On, Scan, Battles, and Closet. The collection also covers Welcome, Onboarding, Sign In, Shop, Stylist, Settings, Style Preferences, Your Photo, Credits, Credit Packs, Credit History, Membership, Try-On History, Saved Shopping, Battle Creation, Account, Support, Privacy, Terms, and Password Reset.

Screens that require an account show their guest entry state. Signed-in Closet items, generated try-on results, and live battle voting require an account or a populated link. The similar-styles route stayed on its loading screen in this session. These are mobile web references for screenshot design.
