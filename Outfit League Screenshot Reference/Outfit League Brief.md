# Outfit League

Outfit League is a fashion discovery, AI virtual try-on, and outfit voting app. The app’s welcome screen sums it up as “Find the fit. Try it on. Let the squad vote.” It helps people discover outfits, preview how clothing might look on their own photo, organize inspiration, and get friends’ opinions before deciding what to wear or buy.

## Features

- **Explore** browses and searches sample looks and products by style, brand, or keyword. Style filters include Trending, Streetwear, Y2K, Minimal, Casual, Vintage, Athleisure, Formal, and Eastern.
- **Try On** pairs a clear full-body photo with a clothing item. Users can add an item or paste a retailer link, then generate an AI preview. Results can lead to saving the look, starting a battle, or shopping similar pieces.
- **Scan** starts with a photo of a look and helps find similar styles. Shopping suggestions can open the retailer’s own product page.
- **Battles** put two looks head to head. Friends can vote through a share link. The voting experience includes a score and space for reactions and comments.
- **Closet** organizes saved clothing and look inspiration privately. Users can save items from photos or links, add tags and notes, revisit generated looks, and bring saved pieces into Try On.
- **Shop and Stylist** help users find pieces and outfit ideas. Style selections inform discovery and recommendations. Shopping includes similar pieces and saved looks.
- **Onboarding** starts with the gap between how an outfit looks online and how it feels in person. It asks about fit frustrations and style, introduces the AI fitting room, and guides users through adding a try-on photo. Guest mode allows sample browsing. The captured web build asks users to sign in to generate a try-on.

## Visual design

The app combines oversized, heavy headlines with compact body text, bright yellow gradients, dark ink, and warm cream surfaces. Large rounded cards, pill-shaped filters, pastel accent panels, and slightly tilted outfit illustrations give it a playful editorial look. The brand mark is a hanger with a sparkle. The main navigation has five tabs, Explore, Try On, Scan, Battles, and Closet. Shared corner radii are 36 for headers, 32 for hero surfaces and the tab bar, 28 for cards, 22 for chips, 20 for search, and 18 for buttons.
